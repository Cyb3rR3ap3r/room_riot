# Curated content review record

This record is intentionally separate from automated content validation. A green validator proves
schema, taxonomy metadata, duplicate detection, length bounds, and prohibited-term screening; it does
not replace an editorial answerability, tone, or safety review.

## Review rules

For every prompt, the reviewer records pass/fail for:

- Correct game and content mode, expected answer shape, and playable round type.
- Clear wording that can be answered without private, identifying, or sensitive information.
- Appropriate length, tone, audience, and risk tags; no coercion, minors, hate, trauma pressure, or
  non-consensual sexual framing.
- A genuinely distinct idea rather than a noun-substitution variant of another prompt.
- After-dark consent framing and adult-only audience where applicable.

Record only prompt IDs and review decisions. Do not collect players' answers or other personal data.
