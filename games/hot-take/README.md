# Hot Take

Hot Take is the second playable Room Riot game. Players submit opinions privately, vote on
anonymous answers, and earn points when their answer receives votes.

Open prompts accept free-form answers. Player-targeted prompts let players choose someone in the
room; the server resolves the player name for display while keeping answer ownership anonymous.

Content lives in `content/*.json`; rules, voting, and scoring live in `src/index.ts`.
